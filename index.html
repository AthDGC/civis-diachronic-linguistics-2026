Where Computation Meets Language History

Discover how machine learning, statistical models, and digital tools are transforming our understanding of linguistic evolution

A New Approach to Historical Linguistics

This programme bridges traditional philological methods with cutting-edge computational techniques. Students engage with large-scale corpora, natural language processing algorithms, and statistical models to uncover patterns in language change.

From Classical to Modern Greek, from Latin to Romance languages, explore how computational methods reveal insights impossible through traditional analysis alone.

Digital Humanities Research

Work with historical texts using Python, R, and specialized NLP libraries to analyze linguistic patterns across centuries.

International Collaboration

Connect with students and faculty from 11 leading European universities in the CIVIS Alliance network.

Hands-On Projects

Complete individual research projects combining linguistic theory with computational methods and present findings to peers.
