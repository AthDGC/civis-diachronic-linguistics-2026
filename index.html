The Venue: Naxos

The Summer School is hosted in the heart of the Cyclades on the island of Naxos. Physical mobility participants will enjoy academic sessions against the backdrop of the iconic Portara (Temple of Apollo) and the Medieval Kastro.

Naxos offers a unique environment where the evolution of the Greek language can be traced from ancient epigraphy to modern dialects.
