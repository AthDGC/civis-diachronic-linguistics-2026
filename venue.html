Naxos, Greece

The Cycladic Heart of the Summer School

About the Venue

The physical mobility phase takes place on the island of Naxos, the largest and most fertile of the Cycladic islands. The school combines intensive academic study with the rich archaeological heritage of the region.

Academic Center

Lectures are held in the historic center of Naxos town, providing an atmosphere that bridges modern linguistic research with ancient history.

Cultural Heritage

Participants will visit the Temple of Apollo (Portara) and the Archaeological Museum of Naxos, which houses significant artifacts from the Cycladic civilization.

Practical Information

Naxos is accessible by ferry from Piraeus (Athens) or by direct flight from Athens International Airport. Detailed travel and accommodation guides will be provided to all admitted students.
