Co-ordinators of the CIVIS BIP Course

Meet the international faculty leading the 2026 edition

Prof. Nikolaos Lavidas

National and Kapodistrian University of Athens

Associate Professor of Diachronic Linguistics. Lead coordinator for the AthDGC Research Network and the CIVIS Blended Intensive Programme.

Prof. Francesca Dell’Oro

University of Bologna

Expert in Ancient Greek Onomastics and Indogermanic linguistics, focusing on the diachronic evolution of epigraphic texts.

Prof. Artemij Keidan

Sapienza University of Rome

Specialist in computational approaches to descriptive linguistics and the integration of AI tools in historical research.

Prof. Ljuba Veselinova

Stockholm University

Expert in linguistic typology and the diachronic evolution of negation systems across diverse language families.

Prof. Maria Isabel Jimenez Martinez

Autonomous University of Madrid

Leading researcher in Latin and Romance diachrony, specifically regarding verb-nominal collocations.

Prof. Tamara Bouso Rivas

University of Santiago de Compostela

Expert in Construction Grammar and the application of corpus-based methods to English historical syntax.
