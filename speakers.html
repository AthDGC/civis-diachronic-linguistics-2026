Co-ordinators of the CIVIS BIP Course

Meet the distinguished international faculty leading the 2026 academic sessions.

Professor Nikolaos Lavidas

Professor of Diachronic Linguistics

National and Kapodistrian University of Athens

Professor Nikolaos Lavidas is a leading figure in the Department of English Language and Literature. His extensive research focuses on historical linguistics, specifically the diachrony of Indo-European languages, and the formal analysis of language change. As the lead coordinator of the Athens Diachronic & Geometric Corpus (AthDGC), he has pioneered digital methods for studying syntactic evolution. He serves as the primary scientific coordinator for the CIVIS Blended Intensive Programmes, bridging traditional philological rigor with modern computational techniques.

Core Expertise

Historical Syntax, Diachronic Corpora, Language Contact, Indo-European Linguistics.

Professor Francesca Dell’Oro

Associate Professor

University of Bologna

Professor Francesca Dell’Oro is an expert in Ancient Greek and Indo-European comparative linguistics. Her research often intersects with digital humanities, specifically in the fields of epigraphy and onomastics. She has conducted landmark studies on the Styra tablets and the diachronic development of local Greek dialects. Within the CIVIS program, she provides critical philological insights into the evolution of ancient texts and their digital preservation.

Core Expertise

Ancient Greek Philology, Epigraphy, Onomastics, Comparative Linguistics.

Professor Artemij Keidan

Associate Professor of Linguistics

Sapienza Università di Roma

Professor Artemij Keidan teaches General Linguistics and Sanskrit at Sapienza. His research bridges the gap between ancient grammatical traditions and contemporary computational models. He is a primary advocate for the multistratal approach to descriptive linguistics, exploring how Artificial Intelligence can be used to formalize and analyze historical linguistic structures.

Core Expertise

Computational Linguistics, AI in Humanities, Sanskrit, General Linguistic Theory.

Professor Ljuba Veselinova

Professor of Linguistics

Stockholm University

Professor Ljuba Veselinova is a prominent typologist whose research focuses on language change and grammaticalization. She is internationally recognized for her work on the diachrony of negation systems and cyclical models of language change. Her research utilizes large-scale cross-linguistic data to understand how grammatical structures emerge and shift across different language families.

Core Expertise

Linguistic Typology, Grammaticalization, Diachrony of Negation.

Professor Maria Isabel Jimenez Martinez

Professor of Ancient and Modern Greek

Universidad Autónoma de Madrid

Professor Maria Isabel Jimenez Martinez specializes in the diachrony of Greek and Latin, focusing on the historical transition into the Romance family. Her recent research examines verb-nominal collocations and their structural stability over long periods. She brings a deep comparative perspective to the program, linking classical roots to modern linguistic behavior.

Core Expertise

Latin Diachrony, Romance Linguistics, Comparative Syntax, Collocations.

Professor Tamara Bouso Rivas

Faculty Researcher

University of Santiago de Compostela

Professor Tamara Bouso Rivas focuses on English historical linguistics through the lens of cognitive and constructionist models. Her work employs corpus-based methods to investigate syntactic shifts in English, particularly the development of complex "Objoid" constructions. She is a specialist in applying Construction Grammar (CxG) to diachronic linguistic data.

Core Expertise

Construction Grammar, English Historical Syntax, Corpus Linguistics.
