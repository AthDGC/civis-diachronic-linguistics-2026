Co-ordinators of the CIVIS BIP Course

Meet the international team of experts leading the 2026 curriculum

Professor Nikolaos Lavidas

Professor of Diachronic Linguistics

National and Kapodistrian University of Athens

Nikolaos Lavidas is Professor at the Department of English Language and Literature. His research focuses on historical linguistics, specifically the diachrony of Indo-European languages. He is the lead coordinator of the Athens Diachronic & Geometric Corpus (AthDGC) and has published extensively on the evolution of syntax, argument structure, and the relationship between language contact and language change. He serves as the primary scientific coordinator for the CIVIS Blended Intensive Programmes in Linguistics.

Core Expertise

Historical Syntax, Language Change, Diachronic Corpora, Indo-European Linguistics.

Professor Francesca Dell’Oro

Associate Professor

University of Bologna

Francesca Dell’Oro is a specialist in Ancient Greek and Indo-European linguistics. Her work frequently intersects with digital humanities, particularly in the study of epigraphy and onomastics. She has conducted groundbreaking research on the Styra tablets and the diachronic development of local dialects in Ancient Greece. Her contribution to the program focuses on the evolution of Greek from a philological and epigraphic perspective.

Core Expertise

Ancient Greek Onomastics, Epigraphy, Indo-European Comparative Linguistics.

Professor Artemij Keidan

Associate Professor of Linguistics

Sapienza Università di Roma

Artemij Keidan teaches General Linguistics and Sanskrit at Sapienza. His research bridges the gap between traditional grammar and modern computational models. He is a leading voice in applying "multistratal" approaches to descriptive linguistics in the age of Artificial Intelligence. His work explores how AI can assist in the structural analysis of historical texts and the formalization of ancient grammatical traditions.

Core Expertise

Computational Linguistics, AI in Humanities, Sanskrit, General Linguistic Theory.

Professor Ljuba Veselinova

Professor of Linguistics

Stockholm University

Ljuba Veselinova is a prominent typologist whose research focuses on language change and grammaticalization. She is internationally recognized for her work on the diachrony of negation and the development of cyclical models (such as the Jespersen and Croft cycles). Her research utilizes large-scale cross-linguistic data to understand the universal tendencies of language evolution.

Core Expertise

Linguistic Typology, Grammaticalization, Diachrony of Negation.

Professor Maria Isabel Jimenez Martinez

Professor of Ancient and Modern Greek

Universidad Autónoma de Madrid

Maria Isabel Jimenez Martinez specializes in the diachrony of the Greek and Latin languages, with a particular focus on the transition from Latin to the Romance family. Her recent work examines verb-nominal collocations and their stability or change across centuries. She brings a deep comparative perspective to the CIVIS program, linking classical philology to modern linguistic structures.

Core Expertise

Latin Diachrony, Romance Linguistics, Collocations, Comparative Syntax.

Professor Tamara Bouso Rivas

Researcher & Faculty Member

University of Santiago de Compostela

Tamara Bouso Rivas is an expert in English historical linguistics and cognitive linguistics. Her research employs corpus-based methods to investigate the development of specific syntactic constructions in English, such as the "Objoid Construction." She is a specialist in Construction Grammar (CxG) and its application to diachronic data.

Core Expertise

Construction Grammar, Corpus Linguistics, English Historical Syntax.
